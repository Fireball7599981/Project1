Digital Technologies Learning Area

You probably make use of technology in your everyday lives at home, in school and on the go. Through Digital Technology you will develop skills which will be beneficial to you throughout your school career and beyond. Digital Technology courses include aspects of the following: Software packages and their use, hardware, programming, project management. The skills you learn are universal and be transferable to many other subjects and careers can take you anywhere in the world.
